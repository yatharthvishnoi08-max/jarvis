# Jarvis feature and verification register

The Windows laptop hosts the owner's assistant. A session in the owner-facing Jarvis UI is the acceptance entry point for desktop and conversation features. A working helper, passing mock, or other agent's claim is recorded separately from that end-to-end result. Use free service plans; keep credentials on the host.
