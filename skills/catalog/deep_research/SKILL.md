## Deep Research Protocol
1. Break user question into 2-3 focused sub-queries.
2. Execute web_search on each query.
3. Use fetch_web_page on the top 2 authoritative URLs to extract in-depth technical facts.
4. Cross-reference conflicting claims and assemble a cited Markdown dossier.
